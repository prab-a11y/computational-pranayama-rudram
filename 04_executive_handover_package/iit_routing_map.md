# Institutional Routing Matrix & Strategic Contact Targets (IIT Madras)

To ensure this cross-disciplinary data package reaches faculty members equipped to utilize and advance the model, submissions should be routed directly to these research hubs within the IIT Madras ecosystem:

## 1. Department of Humanities & Social Sciences (HSS)
*   **Core Research Focus:** Computational Sanskrit Linguistics, Quantitative Philology, and Oral Prosody Text Mapping.
*   **Actionable Application:** Faculty can integrate the Swift parsing binary into graduate research projects exploring structural syllable density across Vedic literature.

## 2. Centre for Indian Knowledge Systems (CIKS)
*   **Core Research Focus:** Scientific Validation and Modeling of Traditional Shastras using Modern Engineering Tools.
*   **Actionable Application:** Serves as the primary institutional home for the project, providing the academic framework to connect *Śikṣā* phonetics with modern data metrics.

## 3. Department of Biomedical Engineering
*   **Core Research Focus:** Cardiopulmonary Physiology, Diaphragm Strain Modeling, and Heart Rate Variability (HRV) Biofeedback.
*   **Actionable Application:** Researchers can establish laboratory field trials using chest tracking belts and gas monitoring equipment to measure real-time *Ghanapāṭhi* lung variables against our model's PLU predictions.

## 4. Robert Bosch Centre for Data Science and AI (RBCDSAI)
*   **Core Research Focus:** Predictive Analytics, Interdisciplinary Data Modeling, and Zero-Shot Audio Machine Learning.
*   **Actionable Application:** Graduate students can refine the algorithm's time-series analytics and expand the model to parse other oral traditions.
