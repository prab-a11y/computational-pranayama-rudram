### 2. The Mathematical Metric: Prāṇic Load Unit (PLU)
The physiological tax of any given textual line is calculated using the four-factor equation:
$$\text{PLU} = \text{Breath Duration (s)} \times \text{Vocal Intensity (\% MVE)} \times \text{Compound Density (CPS)} \times \text{Repetitions}$$

*   **Vocal Intensity:** Modeled at 85% Maximum Voluntary Effort (MVE) to reflect traditional high-tone lineage delivery.
*   **Compound Density:** Measures Compounds Per Second (CPS) to track the complexity of mechanical glottal shifting.
