# Expert Validation Metrics: Traditional Lineage Interview Blueprint

This qualitative framework contains six explicit queries designed to be presented to traditional *Taittirīya Śākhā Ghanapāṭhis* during field recording sessions. Their oral lineage answers serve as the formal primary validation to back up or refine the computational model's respiratory parameters.

## The Six Core Lineage Queries

1.  **Guru-Śikṣā (Lineage Pacing Rules):** How many distinct, voluntary inhalations were you taught to execute specifically within the boundaries of Namakam Anuvāka 8? Is it traditionally permissible to skip or compress these breath markers to accelerate the overall chant pace?
2.  **Daṇḍa-Niyama (Inhalation Rules):** Is executing a full inspiration at every traditional double-daṇḍa (`॥`) marker strictly mandatory across your lineage, or is it optional? What structural, mental, or physiological errors (*doṣas*) are said to occur if a chanter skips a marker?
3.  **Mahāprāṇa-Kāla (Phonetic Duration):** Does an explosive aspirated consonant (like `bha`, `dha`, or `gha`) require more physical execution time than its unaspirated flat counterpart (like `ba`, `da`, or `ga`)? Based on your master training, should that duration be exactly twice as long, three times as long, or more?
4.  **Visarga-Vidhi (Exhalation Dynamics):** Is a chanter required to completely empty the lungs through a forced abdominal squeeze during the *visarga* of the terminal word `namaḥ`, or should they retain residual reserve volume to prepare for the next line segment?
5.  **Ekādaśa Rudra (Macro Pacing Guidelines):** According to your specific *paramparā* (lineage tradition), what is the absolute minimum duration required to complete a full 11-cycle Ekādaśa Rudra performance?
6.  **Doṣa (Traditional Error Assessment):** What specific physical or cognitive problems happen if someone charms too quickly? Did your teachers pass down explicit warnings regarding rapid pacing?
